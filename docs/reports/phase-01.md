## Open questions

1. **`01` MSG-SEND-002, a label "longer than 128 characters" (Tier 2; also MSG-SEND-003's person reference and `PushTopic`, and `08` section 4 `Email:From:DisplayName`).** Readings: (a) UTF-16 code units; (b) Unicode scalar values; (c) grapheme clusters. Smallest fix: the unit named once in `07` or at each limit. Parked: the 128-character label check of MSG-SEND-002. **Answered by M-016: reading (b).** `00` section 1.5 states the unit once; a lone surrogate fails the check. Built: `TextLength`, the label check of `DeclarationCheck`, and the tests of MSG-SEND-002 AC2 for 128 and 129 scalar values outside the Basic Multilingual Plane and a lone surrogate.
2. **`08` section 4 `Templates:Path`, "A folder" (Tier 2).** Readings: (a) the folder must exist, a host of assembly templates alone making an empty one; (b) a well-formed path is enough, a missing folder holding no template. Smallest fix: a clause in the row. Parked: the check of `Templates:Path`. **Answered by M-016: reading (b).** A path naming a file, or a folder the process cannot read, is outside the rule. Built: the check in `ConfigurationCheck`, the binding in `TemplatesOptions`, and the tests of MSG-OPS-001 AC1 for a file, an unreadable folder, a missing folder, a readable one and the content root.
3. **`08` section 4 `Retry:Factor` "1 to 10" and `Jobs:StallFactor` "2 to 100" (Tier 2).** Readings: (a) whole numbers; (b) decimal ones, as `Sms:DrainFactor` is written "1.5 to 100". Smallest fix: a word in each row. Parked: the two keys' check and binding. **Answered by M-016: reading (b).** Built: both keys in `ConfigurationRules`, `RetryOptions` and `JobsOptions`, and the tests of MSG-OPS-001 AC1 for their ranges, their binding and the invariant culture.
